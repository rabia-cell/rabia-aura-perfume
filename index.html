Our story

Aura began as a one-room studio with a simple rule: every bottle should smell like a place you want to return to. We blend traditional attar craft with modern French-style composition.

Each eau de parfum is macerated for four weeks, then rested before bottling, so the notes settle into one calm, lasting signature.

Questions, answered

How long does a fragrance last?

Our eau de parfum concentration typically lasts 6 to 8 hours on skin, depending on skin type and weather.

Which size should I choose?

Start with 30 ml to test a scent, 50 ml for regular wear, and 100 ml for the best value per millilitre.

How do I make a fragrance last longer?

Apply to moisturised skin at pulse points such as the wrists and neck. Avoid rubbing your wrists together.

Can I buy from this website?

No. Aura Perfumes is a fictional brand and this is a practice project, so no orders are taken and no payments are processed.

How should I store my perfume?

Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. The box is a good home for it.
